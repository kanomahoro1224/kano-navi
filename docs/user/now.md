# Now

The board's first section answers "what's next?".

## Next Up

A ticket shows the next stream or event (a sale stays on the schedule
instead): its artwork, the date and time in
Japan, the same moment in your own time zone, and a countdown in hours and
minutes. While a stream is live the ticket switches to an on-air state. A
bilibili stream opens Kano's bilibili live room. When she goes live on
bilibili without an announced entry, the ticket shows **Live now on
bilibili** with the room's title and a link to watch. Its
label names the activity with an icon when the video or the schedule says it
is gameplay, talk, or singing, and otherwise the kind of stream or video. A
members-only stream keeps its Members only mark beside that label.

When nothing is scheduled, the latest video takes its place, passing over
one that has been made private or removed. A schedule entry
without artwork appears as a compact date ticket with its Auto-detected or
Confirmed label and a link to the stream or its announcement, followed by the
latest video as a small card with its kind and, when it has one, its
gameplay, talk, or song label.

## On Sale

Below the ticket, an On sale row lists up to three merchandise orders,
pre-orders, ticket sales or lotteries that are on sale now, closing soonest
first, or that open within a week. They include goods in the official shop
that Kano takes part in, such as group collections, even when no post
announces them; such a sale is named after its collection in the shop. Each shows when it closes (or opens) in
Japan time and links to the shop or announcement. The row appears only when
there is such a sale. On phones its cards scroll sideways, with the next one
showing at the edge.

## Profile Card

Beside the ticket, the profile card shows Kano's banner, avatar, name,
catchphrase, and counts, together with the latest collection run's outcome
and time, the last successful read of X, YouTube, bilibili and the official
shop, and a reload button. Each count opens its section of the board: upcoming entries the
schedule, posts the post wall, and videos the video shelf.
Its links lead to both X accounts by handle and to the YouTube channel and
bilibili space by their icons.

On phones the card comes first, above the ticket, as a compact identity row:
a short banner strip with the avatar overlapping its lower left edge, as on
wider screens, beside the name, and the catchphrase below them in line with
the links. The role line above the
name (Virtual Artist · ミリプロSONA) and the counts are left out, and the last row spreads the X, YouTube, bilibili
and shop read times across the card, with a dot in the run's state colour and the
reload button at its end, so the schedule follows sooner. The ticket's
countdown sits with its time, and the latest video card puts its labels above
the thumbnail and title.
