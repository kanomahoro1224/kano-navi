<details>
<summary><strong>Upgrade notes</strong></summary>

- The public API only adds a read: `GET /api/posts` without `from` and `to`
  pages through every shown post, up to an optional `until` date and
  optionally for one `account`, instead of answering `400 invalid_range`.
  Range reads are unchanged.
- Public `GET /api/dashboard` and `GET /api/posts` add `accountAvatars`,
  mapping `<source>:<account_key>` to a versioned
  `/media/account-avatar/{id}?v={sha256}` path, or `null` for a hidden
  account; an account without a cached avatar is omitted. The new media path
  serves a ready avatar with the same checks and cache headers as
  `/media/{id}`.
- Startup adds `account_avatars` and `account_avatar_candidates`, and the
  per-account avatar observation order, initialized from existing
  candidates without changing manual and hidden selections.
- X synchronization requests `profile_image_url` in its post reads and
  queues each new author avatar as an X media download, without delaying
  post ingestion.
- Admin adds the `/account-avatars` reads and actions (discover, add a URL,
  upload, mode, select, download, delete, preview and apply to the site
  profile), `POST /profile-media/clear` with `{slot}`, and an optional
  `handle` on `POST /profile-media/discover` for X.

</details>

v0.8.1 gives each X account its own portrait on the board, chosen
automatically from synchronization or by an operator. Recent Posts show
images whole and quotes compactly, the post archive pages through every
shown post, the board drops its small section labels and filter counts, and
the About page is rebuilt around its app cards.

<details>
<summary><strong>Public pages</strong></summary>

- A post shows its X account's own portrait when the board has one, and its
  account's initial when the portrait is hidden or fails to load.
- Recent Posts show a lone image whole in its own shape, a very tall or very
  wide one on a blurred copy of itself, and count images past four on the
  fourth tile. A card's images and quote follow its wording instead of
  sinking to its foot.
- A quoted post shows its author's portrait when the board has one, and on
  the board its first image beside its wording with a count of the rest.
  Past posts and the full post show a quote's images like a post's.
- Weekly schedule, Recent Posts, Recent streams and works, Links, and the
  month, Past posts and Past videos sheets show their titles without a small
  label above them; the weekly schedule shows the week's dates beside its
  title. The board's post and video filters show no counts. The public API
  still returns the counts.
- The footer drops its About link and shows Notes and Back to top as icons.
- On a wide screen the common tags sit under the links, beside the journey
  card.
- Clicking the month or year arrows quickly in the month, Past posts and
  Past videos sheets no longer selects the text beside them.
- A schedule entry's details open its link from the arrow in the bottom
  corner, as the entry's card does, instead of an Open link button on a row
  of its own.
- A release's details on Works keep their width as you switch editions: a
  pane whose contents start or stop scrolling keeps its width.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Operator console</strong></summary>

- Avatars lists every tracked X account (own, related and other) with its
  avatar history. An account is automatic (the newest observed image wins),
  manual (the operator's pick stays across synchronizations) or hidden (the
  board shows its initial). Operators can discover the latest avatar, add an
  image URL or upload one, download, select or delete a candidate, and copy
  an own account's avatar into the site profile.
- Profile can discover an avatar and banner for a chosen X handle, falls back
  to FxTwitter when the X profile page names no image, and can return a slot
  to the built-in image while keeping its candidates.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Synchronization and extraction</strong></summary>

- X synchronization records each post author's avatar for the account.
- Automatic account avatars follow source observation order, so a delayed
  download cannot replace a newer ready avatar. A source returning to an
  earlier cached URL still selects that image.
- An operator's account-avatar download updates every candidate sharing its
  media resource, including pending candidates and refreshed cached images.
  Manual selections and hidden accounts keep their modes.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Runtime and development</strong></summary>

- GitHub Actions splits the Go gate into Go builds (CGO-free contracts and
  Linux/Windows builds) and Go container (service image and read-only smoke),
  running beside Backend. They leave the cgo contracts and vet to Backend and
  keep compilation caches keyed by the Go sources.
- `make go-check` runs formatting, the CGO-free contracts, the platform builds
  and the container smoke; `make go-build-check` and
  `make go-container-check` run its two CI halves.

</details>
