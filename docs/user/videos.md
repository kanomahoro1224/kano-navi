# Videos

**Streams and works** brings together YouTube uploads, reservations, and
bilibili copies of the same work.

## Lead and Reservations

A large lead card shows the maintainer's featured video, or else the newest
cover, original song, or premiere, with its date, length, and a link for each
platform. Beside it, upcoming reservations appear as date tickets, soonest
first; a reservation that has started reads as on air. Up to three earlier
works remain beside those reservations.

## Archives and Shorts

Below the lead, stream archives appear as a grid of large thumbnails (two rows
of two on phones), followed by a row of Shorts. An **All / Streams / Works /
Shorts** filter shows one kind at a time.

A video that also has a bilibili upload carries a two-platform mark and a link
for each platform. Chinese readers open such past videos on bilibili first;
reservations and live streams always open on YouTube. Video titles appear in
your content language when a translation is available, followed by an **AI**
mark that is lit while the translation shows; select it to see the original
wording and again to bring the translation back.

## Past Videos

**Past videos** (a clock icon on phones) browses every stored YouTube and
bilibili video month by month as a grid of covers, newest first, each with
its title, Japan date and time, and kind. Buttons above the grid narrow the
month to games, talk, songs or everything else, each with its count; the
choice stays while you move between months. A video counts as a game, talk
or song when the maintainer tagged it so, otherwise when its schedule entry
carries that label, and otherwise when an automatic reading of its title
says so; anything else counts as other. Whether a video is a stream, a
premiere or an upload comes from YouTube itself and never from its title.
