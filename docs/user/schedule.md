# Schedule

## Weekly Guide

The weekly schedule is a Monday-first program guide: seven day columns on
wide screens and a day list on phones. Each entry shows its Japan time (or
"time TBA" when only the date is known), its type, a status when it is on
air, TBA, or cancelled, and an icon for whether it is Auto-detected
(sparkles) or Confirmed (check badge). Entries of earlier days, and today's
once they end, fold to their time, activity icon, and the start of their
title; a stream still on air stays whole. The legend under the guide and the
month names both marks, then each activity icon.
The entry's color follows its type, so members-only streams stand apart.
When the announcement, the title, or the maintainer says what the activity
is, an icon and label name it: gameplay, talk, singing, a stream, or an
upload. A song on a premiere or upload reads as music, with a note in place
of the singing microphone. Gameplay, talk, or singing wins over a plain stream or upload, so an
entry and its video under Past videos name the same activity. An activity label
takes the place of a plain stream type, and a members-only, event, or release
entry keeps its type beside it. An entry the maintainer has not confirmed may
carry a note when it looks cancelled.

A sale (merchandise orders, pre-orders, ticket sales and lotteries) appears
on the day it opens and again on the day it closes, each marked Opens or
Closes; when only the deadline is known it appears on that day alone. Both
entries show whether the sale is upcoming, on sale, or over. They never take
the place of the next stream at the top of the board; the
[On sale](now.md#on-sale) list shows the current ones instead. Sales come from
Kano's and her agency's posts and from the official shop, whose goods with
Kano state their order or sale period; a shop sale opens at the time the
shop put it on sale and closes on its last day, and links to the collection
or the product. A post and the shop entry of the same sale show
as one entry.

Click or tap an entry to open its details: the whole title, its Japan time
(and your own time when you are outside Japan), its labels, whether it is
Auto-detected or Confirmed, any cancellation note, and **Open link** for the
stream, video, or post it comes from. The arrow in an entry's bottom corner
opens that link directly. An entry on more than one platform, such as a
stream on YouTube and bilibili at once, shows one link per platform, with
the live one marked; so does an entry on one platform whose announcement is
a post, and the arrow and **Open link** then open that post.

Use the arrows to move between weeks, **Today** to return to the current
week, or the shortcut to the nearest week that has events. When Kano posts a
weekly schedule image on X, it appears beside the guide and opens in full
with a link to the original post.

## Month View

**Month view** opens a month grid with compact entries (dots on phones); an
entry with an activity label starts with its icon, and hovering it names the
activity. On wide screens the grid fills the window, and a day with one or
two entries wraps their titles onto more lines, more again in a tall window.
Move
between months, or select the month name to jump to any month of any year;
the grid keeps its place while a month loads. Click or tap a day to list its entries beside the grid (below
it on phones) with their whole titles, and show that date's week on the
board. On wide screens clicking an entry in the grid lists its day and marks
that entry; on phones a tap shows the day's entries. An entry in the list
opens its details as in the weekly guide.

## Calendar Subscription

**Subscribe** (a calendar-plus icon on phones) adds the schedule to a calendar
app, or copies the subscription link. The calendar covers the past month and
the next three months and follows the language you picked on the site; your calendar app
decides how often it refreshes. Timed entries appear in your calendar's own time
zone, and entries without a time stay single all-day entries on their Japan
date. A sale appears as "Sales open" and "Sales close" entries on those
days. Cancelled entries are marked as cancelled, and uncertain ones as
tentative.

Feed readers can also follow recent videos and reservations through the
board's video feed, which pages advertise to browsers and readers.
