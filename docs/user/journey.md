# Journey

The Journey page follows Kano's public milestones from her first covers in
2010 to joining Milpro SONA in 2026.

## Timeline

Every milestone keeps the precision of its source (a year, a month, a day, or
a range), names the identity she was active under (singer, Hanayori Joshiryo,
Kano Mahoro, MKLNtic, Milpro SONA), and links its strongest public source.
Filter by identity, or jump to a year from the year rail: a side rail on wide
screens and a strip on phones.

## Visual Archive

The visual archive collects the site's own images of each identity and the
covers of each release, the same covers the [Works](works.md) page shows.
Filter by image type and open any image in full to see its dimensions, how
its date was determined, its recorded credit, and links to the original
pages; a cover also links to its release on the Works page. Frames from
streams and videos name where and the moment they were taken from.

Artwork and other third-party images keep their original rights; the site's
code license does not cover them.
