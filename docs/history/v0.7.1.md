> database migration or required environment changes. The Bilibili upload
> chain defaults to `list`, `dynamics`, and `app` in that order.

<details>
<summary><strong>Upgrade notes</strong></summary>

- Admin `GET /sources` reports the Bilibili upload chain in `schedules` with
  `fixed: false`, requiring `BILIBILI_COOKIE` or `BILIBILI_SESSDATA` for
  `dynamics` and `BILIBILI_APP_KEY` and `BILIBILI_APP_SECRET` for `app`;
  `PUT /sources/bilibili/schedule` accepts its `chain`, and the `bilibili`
  step result adds `listing`. If no enabled method can run, the read fails
  with `bilibili_no_method`.
- Admin `GET /videos` adds a public Bilibili upload's title `translation`
  summary, as for a YouTube video.
- The translation prompt judges language by a text's own wording rather
  than a quoted song title. An existing Bilibili title detected as Japanese
  keeps that classification until its translation is requested again in
  Videos.

</details>

v0.7.1 makes the Bilibili upload read chain configurable and adds translation
summaries for public Bilibili uploads. The dashboard selects the newest work
across platforms, shows full clamped titles on hover, and presents platform
freshness and profile counts in a compact row.

<details>
<summary><strong>Public pages</strong></summary>

- The video shelf's Latest work is the board's newest cover or premiere on
  any platform, including a Bilibili-only cover.
- Hovering a video or schedule title that its card cuts to two lines shows
  the whole title.
- The profile card shows each platform's last successful read on hover; an
  icon turns amber only when that platform's read is a day old, not when a
  single collection run fails.
- The profile card's counts use small icons with numbers on the same row as
  the platform icons, including on phones.
- The About page adds GPT-6-Astra to the development model credits.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Operator console</strong></summary>

- Sources / Bilibili uploads shows the upload read's three methods in its
  method chain: the upload list, the signed-in dynamics listing, and the
  official client's listing. Each has a switch and up/down controls; a
  method whose credential is unavailable is disabled and names the required
  environment variable.
- Videos shows title translations for public Bilibili uploads, which can be
  corrected or requested again as for a YouTube video's.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Synchronization and extraction</strong></summary>

- Bilibili upload reads try enabled methods in the timer's chain order, so
  the official client's listing can run first or be disabled as a fallback.
  During captcha backoff, only the official client's listing is read, and
  only when the chain enables it.

</details>
