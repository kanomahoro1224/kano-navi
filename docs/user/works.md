# Works

The Works page collects Kano's discography from the first covers in 2010 to
the latest releases. The top of the page (on a phone, just under the title) shows the acoustic cover trilogy
_one_, _two_ and _three_ as the girl from each cover on a small landscape of
rolling hills, each before a disc in that album's colour; select a figure to
open the album. Like a record, the scene has two sides: side A keeps album
order, and side B leads with _two_. The small record at the top right of the scene flips
it, and the page remembers the side on that device. A note there says the sections do not strictly
follow who holds each work's rights and that, with this many works, some
details may be wrong, and welcomes corrections and feedback on GitHub; its
link opens a correction form that asks for the work, what is wrong and a
source.
Under it, the page names the date its releases and uploads cover; later
works are added when the collection is next updated.
**Official discography**, beside the years at the top, opens the official
site's list. Pick a section with
the tabs below, each counting its works; the address remembers the section,
so a link can open it directly.

## Own Releases

Albums, singles and doujin works released under Kano's own name or her
units' (MKLNtic and Weekend Paradise), shown as a shelf of covers by year,
newest first. Filter the shelf by format (albums, singles and EPs, doujin)
or show only the unit works, and switch to oldest first with the order icon
at the end of the filters (on a phone, the filters scroll sideways). Each cover is marked
with its format, with **Digital** when it was released for download or
streaming, with its number of editions when there are several, and with
**Cancelled** when it never went on sale. Pointing at a cover lifts its
record out of the sleeve; where the collection has a photo of the real disc,
that disc is the record. A work released without a cover shows its disc,
and the same disc still slides out. A work sold only as a download, or
cancelled before its CD went on sale, has no disc, so its record is the
cover printed on one; a few CDs with no photo of their disc show it the
same way.

Open a work to see it in full: its cover and record over a wash of the
cover's colours, the release date, label, and counts of tracks, editions and
tracks also posted online, then the tracklist beside the chosen edition's
details, or for a work sold in one version its type, date, label and catalog
number. A track also posted to a video site has a button for each site,
marked when the video is for YouTube channel members only. A work sold in several
editions, such as a limited edition with a DVD and a standard edition, lets
you switch between them, and opens on the edition with the most disc photos,
so a CD comes before its download and a limited edition before a store set
that bundles it with a bonus disc; each edition shows its own cover, catalog number,
JAN, price, format and packaging, what comes in the package, the store
bonuses shops gave with it, where it can be streamed or downloaded, and
its own tracklist with every disc. Where the
collection has a photo of a disc, it sits beside that disc in the tracklist,
so a limited edition with a CD and a DVD shows both discs; select one to
view it in full. The record in the header is the chosen edition's first
disc; when the edition has photos of two or more discs, small round photos
under the cover put another disc on the record, which slides back into the
sleeve and comes out as that disc, marked in the tracklist. Store bonuses for any edition are listed after the edition's own. Select a bonus
image or the large cover to view it in full with its size and where the file
came from; an edition with several covers shows a thumbnail of each. Some covers
are copies from a shop, database or collector rather than the publisher's
own file; these are marked as third-party files. A scan or photo of
someone's own copy names who made it, with a link to their profile where
they have one. A photo from a second-hand listing names the marketplace but
does not link to the seller.

## Featured On

Compilations, other artists' albums and game or anime music with tracks Kano
sang, wrote lyrics for, voiced or guested on. Each entry shows its label,
event and catalog number, what Kano did, and the tracks she is on. **Full
tracklist** lists every track with hers highlighted. The title links to the
database or shop page the record follows. Filter by vocals, lyrics or other
parts, or search titles, tracks and circles.

## Uploads

The songs Kano posted to niconico, YouTube and bilibili, YouTube
members-only posts included: covers, originals, MVs and project songs, as a
grid of covers by year, or on a phone a list with each thumbnail beside its
title. An upload shows the thumbnail of one of its videos once this site
has saved it, and otherwise a printed sleeve with its title. A
recording posted to several sites is one entry with a small mark for each
site beside its date;
members-only videos are marked **Members only**. Where the site knows the
video, the entry adds its length; a video nobody can watch any more is
marked **No longer watchable** and its site mark is dimmed. An entry names
the original artist and any partners. A recording that albums later
included has a disc at the end of its title, with their count when there
are several; select it to list those albums. An upload that sings many
songs in one video has a list mark there with the number of songs; select
it to see them in order.
**Not on an album** keeps only the uploads no album includes, and **Members
only** keeps the members-only posts. Search by song, artist or partner.

The list runs to the date the page header names. Songs posted after it
appear above the list under **Newer uploads**, newest first: song uploads
and premieres the site finds by itself are marked **Found automatically**,
and those the site's editor adds are marked **Added by hand**. Each shows
its thumbnail once the site has saved it, otherwise a printed sleeve, with a
mark for each site it is on. Two rows show at first, and the button
under them opens the rest, up to the newest 48. Newer uploads take no part in the
filters, search or counts, and they move into the list when the collection is next
updated. Songs posted only where this site does not look, such as niconico,
wait for that update.

## Dates and Sources

Dates are the Japan date of first release or upload; an edition that came
out later shows its own date. Names of works, people, stores, editions and
bonus items appear as the releases write them, mostly in Japanese; the words
around them, such as what an image shows, formats, packaging, credits and
track notes, follow the interface language. Covers and works belong to their
rights holders.
