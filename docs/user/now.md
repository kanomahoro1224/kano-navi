# Now

The board's first section answers "what's next?".

## Next Up

A ticket shows the next stream or event (a sale stays on the schedule
instead): its artwork, the date and time in
Japan, the same moment in your own time zone, and a countdown in hours and
minutes. While a stream is live the ticket switches to an on-air state. A
bilibili stream opens Kano's bilibili live room. A stream on YouTube and
bilibili at once is one ticket with a link to each platform; the platform
that is live is marked and opened first. When she goes live on bilibili while
a YouTube stream is on air, that stream's ticket gains its bilibili link.
When she goes live on bilibili without any entry, the ticket shows **Live
now on bilibili** with the room's title and a link to watch. Its
label names the activity with an icon when the video or the schedule says it
is gameplay, talk, or singing, and otherwise the kind of stream or video. A
members-only stream keeps its Members only mark beside that label, and its
cover adds YouTube's members star beside the YouTube mark.

When nothing is scheduled, the latest video takes its place, passing over
one that has been made private or removed. A schedule entry
without artwork appears as a compact date ticket with its Auto-detected or
Confirmed label and a link to the stream or its announcement, followed by the
latest video as a small card with its kind and, when it has one, its
gameplay, talk, or song label.

## On Sale

The On sale list shows merchandise orders, pre-orders, ticket sales or
lotteries that are on sale now, closing soonest first, or that open within a
week. They include goods in the official shop that Kano takes part in, such
as group collections, even when no post announces them; such a sale is named
after its collection in the shop. Each shows when it closes (or opens) in
Japan time and links to the shop or announcement. The list appears only when
there is such a sale.

On wide screens it fills the shorter side of the top of the board: beside a
ticket with artwork, two sales stack under the profile card; under a compact
date ticket, three run as a row. On narrower screens three follow the profile
card as a row, and on phones the row scrolls sideways, with the next card
showing at the edge. When there are more, an All button with the count opens
every sale in a sheet.

## Profile Card

Beside the ticket, the profile card shows Kano's banner, avatar, name,
catchphrase, and one closing row. On its left, three small counts of
upcoming entries, recent posts and recent videos each name their window on
hover and open their section of the board: the schedule, the post wall and
the video shelf. On its right, X, YouTube, bilibili and official shop icons
show on hover when that platform was last read successfully; a platform not
read for a day turns amber with a small dot.
Its links lead to both X accounts by handle and to the YouTube channel and
bilibili space by their icons.

On phones the card comes first, above the ticket, as a compact identity row:
a short banner strip with the avatar overlapping its lower left edge, as on
wider screens, beside the name, and the catchphrase below them in line with
the links. The role line above the
name (Virtual Artist · ミリプロSONA) is left out, so the schedule follows
sooner. The ticket's
countdown sits with its time, and the latest video card puts its labels above
the thumbnail and title.
