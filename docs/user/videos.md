# Videos

**Streams and works** brings together YouTube uploads and reservations and
bilibili uploads, whether they are copies of a YouTube work or only on
bilibili. A work on both platforms is one video with a link for each.

## Lead and Reservations

A large lead card shows the maintainer's featured video, or else the newest
cover, original song, or premiere, with its date, length, and a link for each
platform. Beside it, upcoming reservations appear as date tickets, soonest
first; a reservation that has started reads as on air. Up to three earlier
works remain beside those reservations.

## Archives and Shorts

Below the lead, stream archives appear as a grid of large thumbnails (two rows
of two on phones), followed by a row of Shorts.

On the board, the section a video sits in names how it was published, so
only a featured stream archive or Short in the lead and an upcoming premiere
say so. Under Past videos each video says how it was published (a stream
archive, a video or a Short, or a premiere until it has aired; an aired
premiere is an ordinary video and shows no such label). Each video also says
what it is about when known: a gameplay, talk or
singing stream, or a game, talk or music video, each with an icon: a
controller for games, a speech bubble for talk, a vocal microphone for
singing (as in the schedule) and a note for music. An **All / Streams / Works /
Shorts** filter shows one kind at a time.

Each cover carries the mark of every platform the video is on: YouTube,
bilibili or both. A video only YouTube channel members can watch adds
YouTube's members star beside the YouTube mark, and a **Members only** chip
beside its labels. A video on both also has a link for each platform. Chinese
readers open such past videos on bilibili first;
reservations and live streams always open on YouTube. A bilibili-only video
opens on bilibili; a bilibili live replay is a stream archive, and a new
bilibili upload appears once the board knows whether it is a replay. Video titles appear in
your content language when a translation is available, followed by a
translation mark that is lit while the translation shows; select it to see
the original wording and again to bring the translation back. The mark says
where the translation came from: a translate icon with **AI** for an AI
translation, with the YouTube or bilibili logo for a translation the platform
provides, and alone for one written by the board's editor.

A video that not everyone can watch says so beside its labels: **Members
only** in green, as on YouTube, or a grey **Private** or **Removed**. A
private or removed video stays listed but no longer opens on YouTube; a
bilibili copy, when there is one, still opens, and without one the cover
fades.

## Past Videos

**Past videos** (a clock icon on phones) lists every stored YouTube and
bilibili video in one scrolling list, newest first, as a grid of covers, each
with its title, Japan date and time, and kind. Each month has a header with
its number of videos that stays at the top while you scroll through it, and
older videos load as you reach the end. The month name above the list follows
the scroll; click it to jump to any month that has videos, and use Latest to
return to the newest. Buttons above the list, with the same icons, narrow it
to games, talk, songs or everything else, each with its count across all
months. A video counts as a game, talk
or song when the maintainer tagged it so, otherwise when its schedule entry
carries that label, and otherwise when an automatic reading of its title
says so; anything else counts as other. Whether a YouTube video is a stream,
a premiere or an upload comes from YouTube itself and never from its title.
bilibili does not say whether an upload replays a stream, so the board
decides from the title, the length and recent bilibili broadcasts, with an
automatic reading or the maintainer settling the rest.
